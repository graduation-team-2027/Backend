# Outstanding Enrollment and Privacy Requirements

**Project:** AISmokingDetection
**Owner:** BE-A
**Status:** Pending team agreement

This document records enrollment and privacy requirements that still need clarification. Listing an item here does not mean that it has been approved or implemented.

## 1. Enrollment Requirements

The team needs to agree on:

- The information required to register employees and supervisors.
- Which enrollment fields are mandatory.
- How a person's identity is verified before enrollment.
- How reference images are collected and linked to the correct person.
- Which roles may create, update, deactivate, or review enrollment records.
- How duplicate enrollment records are detected and handled.
- How enrollment changes are recorded for auditing.

## 2. Privacy Requirements

The team needs to define:

- How employees and supervisors are informed about camera-based monitoring.
- The applicable legal basis and any required notice, consent, or authorization before collecting or processing personal or biometric data.
- Which roles may access personal information, reference images, and identity-related records.
- How reference images and related identity data are protected in storage and during transmission.
- How long each category of data is retained.
- The process for correcting, exporting, or deleting data when required and legally permitted.
- Whether access to sensitive data and administrative changes are logged and audited.
- How unknown identities are handled without automatically linking them to an enrolled person.

## 3. Open Questions for Team Agreement

1. What exact fields are required for employee and supervisor enrollment?
2. Who is authorized to approve enrollment?
3. What is the agreed process for collecting and storing reference images?
4. Which roles can view, add, update, or delete personal and biometric data?
5. What retention period applies to reference images and identity records?
6. What happens to enrollment data when a person leaves the organization or a valid deletion request is received?
7. Who is responsible for reviewing and approving the privacy requirements?

## 4. Current Status and Next Steps

- **Documented:** The outstanding questions listed above.
- **Pending:** Team agreement on enrollment fields, permissions, identity verification, data handling, and retention.
- **Not claimed:** No requirement in this document is considered implemented or approved solely because it appears here.

**Next step:** Review these questions with the team, agree on the applicable requirements, and update the API contract and implementation tasks accordingly.
